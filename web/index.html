ShelfCart

Shop any image, change it, buy the real thing

Shop any image.

Paste a screenshot, drop a picture here, or choose a file. ShelfCart finds what is in it and shows real products you can buy from Shopify stores.

Cmd V to paste. On a Mac, Ctrl Shift Cmd 4 copies a screenshot.

Choose an image

With the Chrome extension, right-click any image on any site and pick "Shop this image".

Use the camera instead

Pan slowly across a shelf, a desk or a bag. A video file is sampled into 5 frames in your browser.

    Who does what
    Gemini 4 Hydrogen
    finds the items in the image and suggests ways to change them.
    Shopify Catalog
    is searched across all merchants for each item. Prices, stock and discounts are live.
    Decision 1
    decides in about a second which listings are an exact match.
    Gemini 4 Neon
    takes a careful second look and picks the closest similar item, saying how it differs.
    Gemini image model
    redraws the picture with one item changed. Those are generated previews, not product photos.
    You
    choose every listing, press the button that builds a cart, and pay on Shopify's own checkout page. ShelfCart never pays.
